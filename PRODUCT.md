# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People evaluating Benjamin Falk's engineering work, including potential collaborators, clients, employers, and technical peers.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that quickly establishes Benjamin as a practical software engineer working across AI, data, full-stack systems, computer vision, sound and signal processing, and cybersecurity. Success means a visitor understands his current role, technical range, and strongest work, then has an obvious path to view projects or get in touch.

## Positioning

Benjamin combines research-led Computer Engineering with software that operates in real settings: AI and data pipelines, API integrations, full-stack delivery, client work, technical communication, and products he owns.

## Operating Context

The site is a public GitHub Pages portfolio. Visitors may arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, a job or client conversation, and commonly browse on mobile as well as desktop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only; it must remain compatible with GitHub Pages.
- The site covers Home, About, Work, and Contact.
- The home logo keeps the existing Rickroll link while receiving a new visual identity.
- Sound examples and audio-demo navigation are removed; sound and signal engineering remain represented as a technical capability.
- Current facts: 9th-semester MSc Computer Engineering student at Aalborg University, specializing in AI, Vision and Sound; internship at Ambolt AI doing the same applied AI and software work previously described as student work.
- Work must include Cybervagten (owned by Benjamin), the Hørning Auto Teknik client website, and the 8th-semester project "Automatic Censoring of Profanities During Streaming of Media."
- The Work page keeps three explicit collections: Semester Projects 1–9, Side Projects, and professional Work.
- P9 represents the current Ambolt AI internship: AI workflows and integration using big data in football.
- A small, playful interactive assistant should provide occasional personality without obstructing the portfolio.
- The home-page field sheet is a hands-on engineering artifact: its signal trace draws on load, then its four nodes become draggable.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Benjamin Falk.
- Modern and professional, with visible software craft and a small playful streak.
- Keep the portfolio direct, credible, and technically specific.
- Preserve the concepts "Sound & Signals", "Computer Vision", "AI Engineering", and "Software Engineering" with their existing meaning.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing portfolio copy and project links in `index.html`, `about.html`, and `work.html`.
- Ambolt AI and Aalborg University logo assets in the repository.
- LinkedIn profile supplied by Benjamin and reviewed on 2026-10-29: 9th-semester MSc status; AI, data, software, API integration, full-stack, and LLM experience; student ambassador roles.
- Public repositories and sites supplied by Benjamin: the P8 profanity-censoring project and Hørning Auto Teknik. Cybervagten is represented by its public website; its repository is private.
- No testimonials, client metrics, revenue claims, or confidential project details are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with current, real work rather than generic self-description.
2. Show breadth through specific projects and responsibilities.
3. Make technical depth understandable to non-specialists.
4. Keep personality in interaction details, not at the expense of clarity.
5. Stay fast, accessible, responsive, and dependency-light.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio should support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, semantic headings, and touch targets suitable for mobile use.
